# Lightweight 3D-Printed Bridge

Additive Manufacturing Processes final project: support a 20 lb central load for at least one second with minimal material. The course report documents FDM in PLA, iterative CAD/ANSYS work, print orientation and manual supports.

## Outcome and measurement provenance
Ethan reports a finished structure under 12 g, with 3 g of supports removed before loading, and the still-standing course record. These are author-supplied claims. The report is internally inconsistent: its abstract states 12.29 g while its final-results narrative and table state 17.1 g. No support subtraction, exact final mass or numerical stress result has been inferred. The website explicitly distinguishes these sources.
