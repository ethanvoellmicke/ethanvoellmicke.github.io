UMass Amherst ASME design team · 2024–2025 · IAM3D Challenge.

This portfolio section documents the team project. Ethan Voellmicke is listed as a contributor in the team's March 25, 2025 design report. It does not attribute the entire design or analysis to one person.

No measured flight speed, flight endurance, competition placing, or final integrated payload performance is claimed.
